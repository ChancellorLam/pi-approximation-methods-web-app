The Ramanujan-Sato series is a remarkable infinite series that converges very rapidly to pi, adding about 8 new correct digits of pi per iteration. It builds on the work of the Indian mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan, who possessed incredible natural talent and intuition in mathematics during the early 20th century. This series was later extended by other mathematicians, including those who developed the Sato series.

The beauty of the Ramanujan-Sato series lies in its deep connections to advanced areas of mathematics, creating terms that quickly add up to pi.

Unlike earlier series, the Ramanujan-Sato series reaches an accurate value of pi with far fewer terms. Because of its fast convergence, it forms the basis of the most commonly used algorithms for high-precision calculations of pi today.
